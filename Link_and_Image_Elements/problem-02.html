College Opens New Innovation Center

New facility gives students space for technology projects and collaboration

Gla University officially opened its new Innovation Center on 20 August 2026. The center is designed to give students a dedicated place to work on practical projects.

The facility is located on the main college campus beside the central library. It includes project rooms and shared work areas where students from different departments can collaborate.

The opening ceremony took place on 20 August 2026 and was attended by students, teachers, alumni and local community representatives. The event included demonstrations of student-built technology projects.

The center was created because the college wanted to connect classroom knowledge with practical problem-solving. College officials said the facility should make collaboration easier and encourage students to turn ideas into working projects.
