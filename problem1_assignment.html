GLA UNIVERSITY Greater Noida

About My University:

    
    GLA University Greater Noida is an off-campus centre of GLA University located at
    15A, Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh – 201310. 
    The campus is designed with a focus on industry-integrated education, 
    practical learning and developing skills that are relevant to modern industries.


Courses Offered at GLA Greater Noida

    2.B.Tech in CSE – Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
    3.B.Tech in Electronics & Computer Engineering
    4.AI-Enabled BBA
    5.AI-Enabled MBA (Hons.)
    6.MCA – Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Campus Life at GLA Greater Noida


    Campus life at GLA Greater Noida is designed to provide students with opportunities beyond classroom learning. 
    Students can participate in cultural activities, sports, clubs, events and 
    other activities that help develop communication, teamwork and leadership skills.
    The campus promotes practical learning through laboratories, projects, hackathons, case studies, industry immersion and internships.
