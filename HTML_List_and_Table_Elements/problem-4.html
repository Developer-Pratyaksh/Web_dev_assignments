Laptop Comparison Guide

Choosing the right laptop is important for students. A good laptop should provide suitable performance, storage, battery life and display quality according to the student's requirements.

General Buying Factors

  • Processor
  • RAM
  • Storage
  • Display
  • Battery
  • Warranty

How to Choose Your Laptop

  1. Decide your main purpose.
  2. Choose a suitable processor.
  3. Select enough RAM for your work.
  4. Choose suitable SSD storage.
  5. Check display quality and size.
  6. Compare battery life.
  7. Check warranty and price.

Technical Terms

RAM
Memory used by the computer to temporarily store data being used.
SSD
A fast storage device used to store files and applications.
CPU
The main processor that performs calculations and instructions.
GPU
A processor designed mainly for graphics and visual processing.
Refresh Rate
The number of times the display refreshes every second.
Battery Capacity
The amount of energy a laptop battery can store.
