Annual College Fest 2026

Welcome to the Annual College Fest 2026 of GLA University Greater Noida. The fest provides students with opportunities to participate in cultural, technical, sports and creative activities.

Fest Introduction

Annual College Fest 2026 is a three-day student event featuring competitions, cultural programs, technical activities and entertainment. Students from different groups can participate according to the rules of each event.

Registration Process

  1. Visit the official registration page.
  2. Read the competition details.

  4. Enter your student details.
  5. Submit the registration form.

General Fest Rules

  • Students must carry their university identity card.
  • Participants must report before the event begins.
  • Students must follow instructions given by coordinators.
  • Participants must maintain discipline.
  • Participants should respect other students and staff.
  • Any violation of rules may lead to disqualification.

Registration deadline: 30 September 2026

Important Terms

Open Event
An event in which eligible students can participate.
Team Event
An event where participants compete as a group.
Individual Event
An event where one participant competes individually.
Registration
The process of entering participant information for an event.
Coordinator
The person responsible for managing an event.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition.
