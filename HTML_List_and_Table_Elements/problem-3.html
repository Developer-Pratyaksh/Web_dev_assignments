Academic Rules

  • Students must attend classes regularly.
  • Students must carry their identity card.
  • Students should maintain discipline.
  • Students should submit assignments on time.
  • Students must follow examination instructions.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A class in which the faculty explains theoretical concepts.
Lab
A practical session where students perform experiments or programs.
Tutorial
A session focused on solving questions and clearing doubts.
Internal Assessment
Evaluation conducted during the semester.
Practical
A session where students demonstrate practical knowledge.
