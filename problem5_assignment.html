Science Notes

Important concepts and formulas for quick revision.

Chemistry

Water: H2O

Carbon Dioxide: CO2

Oxygen: O2

Hydrogen: H2

Sulphuric Acid: H2SO4

Ammonia: NH3

Chemical formula tells us which elements are present in a compound.

Chemistry helps us understand the substances around us.

Physics

Speed: Speed = Distance / Time

Force: F = ma

Work: W = F × s

Power: P = W/t

Einstein's Equation: E = mc2

Area: m2

Volume: m3

Force is an important concept in physics.

Physics helps us understand how the world around us works.

Biology

Cell: The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

Photosynthesis: Green plants prepare their food using sunlight, carbon dioxide and water.

DNA: DNA contains genetic information of living organisms.

Photosynthesis is an important process in plants.

Biology helps us understand living organisms.
