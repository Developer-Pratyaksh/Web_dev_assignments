Event Overview

GLA University Greater Noida organizes academic, career and student-development activities. This assignment models a career and education fair portal for students and job seekers.

Objective: connect participants with opportunities in internships, placements, higher education, scholarships and career development.

Prepare questions, carry your resume and explore opportunities relevant to your goals.

Participating Organizations

Technology Sector

Technology organizations may offer software, data, cloud and engineering opportunities.

Business and Consulting Sector

Business organizations may offer management, consulting, finance and operations opportunities.

Preparation Guide

  • Prepare an updated resume.
  • Research organizations before attending.
  • Prepare a short self-introduction.
  • Carry copies of important documents.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters and educators.
  • Dress appropriately for professional interactions.

Preparation Tips

Reach the venue early and check the schedule before selecting sessions.

Tip: Keep your resume concise and highlight relevant projects and skills.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register for the fair.
  2. Check the participating organizations.
  3. Choose sessions.
  4. Attend presentations and interactions.
  5. Ask questions.
  6. Follow up with relevant organizations.
