Student Terms & Rules

Important rules and guidelines for all students.

General Rules

Rule 1: Students must maintain proper discipline and respectful behaviour inside the university campus.

Rule 2: Students must carry their valid student identity card whenever they are on campus.

Rule 3: Students should keep classrooms and university premises clean.

Students are expected to respect teachers, staff members and other students.

Attendance

Rule 4: Students must attend their classes regularly and maintain the required attendance percentage.

Students should avoid unnecessary absence from classes.

Regular attendance helps students understand the subjects properly and perform better in examinations.

Examination

Rule 5: Students must carry their required examination documents and identity card.

Rule 6: Students must not use unfair means during examinations.

Using unfair means may result in disciplinary action.

Students should follow all instructions given by the examination staff.

Assignment Submission

Rule 7: Students must submit assignments before the given deadline.

Late submission may result in marks being deducted.
Students should check the submission instructions carefully.

Submitting work on time shows responsibility and discipline.

Discipline

Rule 8: Students must follow university disciplinary guidelines.

Rule 9: Damaging university property is not allowed.

Serious disciplinary violations may lead to penalties.

Every student is responsible for maintaining a positive learning environment.

Important Notice

Students should regularly check their university notices for important information about classes, examinations and assignments.

Following the rules helps create a safe and respectful environment for everyone.
