About GLA University Greater Noida

GLA University Greater Noida is an industry-integrated campus at 15A, Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida - 201310, Uttar Pradesh.

The campus focuses on industry-integrated education, experiential learning, projects, industry exposure and career preparation.

Application Process

  1. Register online.
  2. Pay the registration fee.
  3. Appear for GLAET where applicable.
  4. Check the GLAET result.
  5. Appear in the interview if shortlisted.
  6. Pay the token amount.
  7. Upload the required documents.
