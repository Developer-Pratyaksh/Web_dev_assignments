Mathematics Formula Sheet

A collection of important formulas for quick revision.

Algebra

1. Square of Sum:

(a + b)2 = a2 + 2ab + b2

2. Square of Difference:

(a - b)2 = a2 - 2ab + b2

3. Difference of Squares:

a2 - b2 = (a + b)(a - b)

4. Sum of Cubes:

a3 + b3 = (a + b)(a2 - ab + b2)

Geometry

Area of Square: a2

Area of Rectangle: l × b

Area of Triangle: 1/2 × b × h

Area of Circle: πr2

These formulas are commonly used in geometry problems.

Mensuration

Volume of Cube: a3

Volume of Cuboid: l × b × h

Surface Area of Cube: 6a2

Volume of Cylinder: πr2h
