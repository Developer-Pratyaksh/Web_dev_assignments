Welcome to GLA University, where academic excellence meets real-world success.
With a legacy of over 25 years, we provide a vibrant ecosystem designed to transform ambitious students into industry-ready professionals.
Whether you are aiming for engineering, management, pharmacy, or applied sciences, your journey to greatness starts here.

The campus is designed to provide students with professional education, practical learning, and industry exposure in the Delhi-NCR region. 


The university offers programs such as B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, 
B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, 
B.Tech in Electronics and Computer Engineering, BBA, MBA, and MCA.

Its academic approach emphasizes project-based learning, internships, live projects, industry interaction, and practical skill development.
GLA University is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and holds a NAAC A+ accreditation. 
The university also highlights its industry collaborations, experienced faculty, modern laboratories, student clubs, cultural activities, 
and sports facilities as important parts of campus life.
